Title: Demo Ingredient Quality Requirements
DocumentType: QUALITY_REQUIREMENT
Version: 1.0
Fictional demo quality requirements.

Every delivered ingredient must match the purchase-order material code, declared quantity, and
sealed package count. The warehouse records visible damage before receiving. A lot with a missing
identifier, broken seal, contamination, or material mismatch is placed on hold and is not received
into usable inventory. Procurement coordinates disposition with the supplier. The system does not
support partial receiving in V1, so the full order must be accepted or the receiving action deferred.

Question: Which defects require a delivered lot to be placed on hold?
Expected basis: missing identifier, broken seal, contamination, or material mismatch.
