Title: SupplyPilot Demo Procurement SOP
DocumentType: PROCUREMENT_SOP
Version: 1.0
Fictional demo procedure.

The production planner records demand. Procurement staff review inventory, incoming ORDERED
purchase orders, and the recommendation snapshot before creating a DRAFT purchase order. A
procurement manager may approve or reject only a DRAFT. Procurement staff may mark an APPROVED
order as ORDERED. Warehouse staff receive only an ORDERED order. Receiving increases inventory,
creates a PURCHASE_RECEIVE stock transaction, and changes the order to RECEIVED in one transaction.
No role may skip a lifecycle state through the user interface.

Question: Which role receives an ORDERED purchase order?
Expected basis: warehouse staff.
